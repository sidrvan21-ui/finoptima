# FinOptima Vendor Management and Shadow IT Playbook
**Document owner:** Group Procurement | **Version:** 2.4

Shadow IT is any subscription billed to a FinOptima legal entity with weak usage, duplicate capability, or no named buyer. This file is **procurement action**. Internal Audit’s idle definition lives in `audit_framework.md` Rule 2.1 — do not confuse the two.

## 3. Commercial actions

### Rule 3.1 — Idle product cancellation window
A subscription with **zero meaningful logins for 30 consecutive days** should be placed on the **cancellation or downgrade** queue before the next invoice. Procurement owns outreach to the vendor. This looks like Rule 2.1 but the owner is Procurement, not Internal Audit.

### Rule 3.2 — Duplicate capability
Two tools in the same job family (e.g. two project-management products, two BI platforms) with combined ARR over **$20,000** require a keep/kill decision within 30 days of detection.

### Rule 3.3 — Renewal blockade
If a vendor renews within **60 days** and either Rule 3.1 or Rule 3.2 applies, auto-renewal is blocked until a buyer signs in FinOptima HITL.

### Rule 3.4 — Preferred-vendor deviation
Net-new SaaS over **$5,000 ARR** that is not on the preferred-vendor list needs a one-page deviation (security review + commercial reason) before PO.

### Rule 3.5 — India and Ireland local tools
Local-entity tools (e.g. Zoho, Freshworks) are allowed if they stay under **$10,000 ARR** and have a named owner. They are still subject to Rule 3.1.
