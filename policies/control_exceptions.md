# FinOptima Control Exceptions and Temporary Waivers
**Document owner:** VP Finance + Chief Audit Executive | **Version:** 1.3

Exceptions never delete a finding. They only delay a **procurement** action or a **memo dispatch**.

## 5. Waivers

### Rule 5.1 — Time-boxed budget exception
A department may receive a written variance waiver for at most **one calendar month**. The waiver must name the legal entity, cost center, and expiry month. Rule 1.1 / 1.2 still appear on the audit trail.

### Rule 5.2 — Strategic-vendor idle waiver
A P1 idle tool (Rule 2.2) may be waived for 30 days if the CRO or CTO attests a launch is in progress. FinOptima HITL is still required before renewal (Rule 3.3).

### Rule 5.3 — No silent override
No exception may hide a Rule 1.2 overrun from the executive memo. The memo must list the waiver ID.

### Rule 5.4 — India statutory tools
India statutory or payroll systems are exempt from Rule 3.1 cancellation even if idle over 30 days, but Rule 2.1 evidence is still collected.

### Rule 5.5 — Lookalike idle language (do not mis-retrieve)
Staff sometimes quote “30 consecutive days” for FX comments or intercompany delays. Those cases use Rule 4.2 or Rule 4.1, **not** Rule 2.1 or Rule 3.1.
